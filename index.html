Meet the owner

A small stall with a big heart

Namaste, I'm Surendra. In 2026 I set up this little food corner near Ramlila Gate in Aonla, with one simple dream: serve the kind of chaat I grew up loving, the tangy, spicy and slightly sweet taste of Moradabad.

Every recipe is tasted by my family before it reaches your plate. If it isn't good enough for them, it doesn't go on the menu. The LED lights are there so you can find us easily, but the food is why you come back.

Whether it's a quick evening snack or an order for a family function, I treat every plate the same way, with love in every bite.

  • Moradabad-style chaat flavours
  • Everything made fresh, never reheated
  • Home delivery in and around Aonla
  • Special orders for weddings, birthdays and parties
  • Easy QR code payment
